Can you explain recursion in a simple way?

✦
EduMind AI

Of course! Think of recursion as a function solving a smaller version of the same problem again and again.

The most important thing is the base case. It tells the function when to stop.

For example, when calculating factorial:

5! = 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1

The function keeps calling itself with a smaller number until it reaches the stopping condition.
